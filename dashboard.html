ELI5: How The Option Wheel Generates Predictable Cash Flow

Investor Primer

Explain Like I'm 5: Why this systematic strategy gets paid whether the market goes up, sideways, or slightly down.

The 10-Second Summary: Think of the Wheel like getting paid cash up-front to set a limit order on high-quality blue-chip stocks you already want to own at a discount. We never gamble on directional lottery tickets — our bot acts like an insurance company, collecting upfront premiums from the market and letting time work in our favor.
1

Sell Cash-Secured Put

"Get paid to shop." We pick a quality stock trading at $100 and say: "We will buy your shares for $90 next month if it drops, and you pay us $200 cash today just for making this promise."

• Outcome A (Stock stays above $90): The promise expires. We keep the $200 pure cash (100% win) and repeat next cycle.

• Outcome B (Stock drops below $90): We buy 100 shares at $90 — a 10% discount from original price!

2

50% Max Profit Exit

"Bank profits fast & compound." Options decay most rapidly in the first 2-3 weeks. As soon as our $200 premium reaches $100 in profit (+50%), our bot automatically buys it back early.

• Zero Late-Stage Tail Risk: We never wait around for the last pennies during erratic earnings.

• Capital Velocity: Unlocks collateral 2-3 weeks ahead of schedule, doubling the annual trade turnover.

3

Sell Covered Call

"Rent out your shares." If we get assigned shares at $90, we immediately sell a Covered Call at $95: "Pay us another $200 cash today for the right to buy our shares at $95."

• If stock climbs past $95: We sell shares for a +$500 capital gain PLUS keep the $200 cash, resetting to 100% cash.

• If stock stays flat: We keep the shares and sell another weekly call, collecting continuous rental income!

The Asymmetry: We win when the stock rises, stays flat, or drops slightly. Downside is strictly buffered by premium collected.
