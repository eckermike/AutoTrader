• Recovers Collateral: Frees our $500 collateral weeks early so we can redeploy into another winning spread.

Automated 30% Tax Shield

"Zero year-end tax surprises." Every single winning spread instantly transfers 30% of its gain into our Virtual Tax Escrow ledger.
